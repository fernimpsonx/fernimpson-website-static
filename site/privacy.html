Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

I’m Fern Impson, a confidence coach based in Australia. This page explains what personal information I collect through this website and my Confidence Quiz, how it’s stored, and how I use it. I handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What I collect

When you complete the Confidence Quiz and ask for your results, I collect:

  • your first name
  • your email address
  • your quiz result (Mind, Heart or Body)
  • a record that you were shown the notice about occasional emails from me, and the wording of that notice
  • the date and time you submitted the form

Your individual answers to the 12 quiz questions are used in your browser to work out your result and draw your results graph. They are not saved in my records.

If you join the waitlist for the 7-Day Confidence Challenge, I collect your first name, your email address, and the date and time you joined.

If you book a free call, I’ll also receive the details needed to arrange it, such as your name and email address, through my booking calendar.

How I use it

  • to show you your personalised results
  • to understand which area of confidence (Mind, Heart or Body) people find most challenging, so I can make my content and coaching more helpful
  • to let you know when the 7-Day Confidence Challenge opens, if you joined the waitlist
  • to arrange and run a call if you book one
  • to send you occasional emails with confidence tips and offers

Emails from me

When you ask for your quiz results, I may send you occasional emails with confidence tips and offers. When you join the challenge waitlist, I’ll email you about the challenge. Both are explained in the note under each form. There is no box to tick, so by submitting a form you’re telling me you’re happy to hear from me this way.

You can unsubscribe at any time, using the link in any email or by contacting me, and I’ll stop. I won’t sell or rent your details to anyone.

Where your information is stored

This website is hosted by Netlify, and your quiz and waitlist details are stored securely in Netlify Forms, where only I can view them through my account. Netlify’s servers may be located outside Australia, including in the United States, so your information may be stored and processed overseas.

Your name and email address are also sent to MailerLite, the email service I use to send emails and manage my mailing list. MailerLite’s servers may also be outside Australia. You can read their privacy policy on their website.

Other services on this website

  • Google Fonts is used to display the fonts on this website. Your browser requests the fonts directly from Google, which can see your IP address.
  • YouTube is used to show the personal video on your results page. YouTube (Google) may collect information, such as your IP address and viewing activity, and may use cookies when the video loads or plays. Please see Google’s privacy policy for details.
  • Booking calendar: if you book a call, the scheduling service I use will collect the details you enter under its own privacy policy.

I don’t use advertising pixels on this website.

Sharing your information

I only share your information with the service providers listed above, and only so they can provide their service to me. I don’t sell your personal information. I may disclose it if the law requires me to.

How long I keep it

I keep your details for as long as I need them for the purposes above, or until you ask me to delete them. I take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

Your choices and rights

You can ask me at any time to give you a copy of the personal information I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop sending you emails. Please contact me using the details on my website.

If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your information, please tell me first so I can put it right. You can also make a complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

I may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
